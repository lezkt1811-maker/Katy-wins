HOW IT WORKS

    
Each Kalshi contract pays $1 if it's right and $0 if it's wrong. If you pay 62¢, you need it to happen more than about 62% of the time (plus the fee) just to break even.

    
The market price is the crowd's guess. On its own, a screenshot can't tell you which bet is underpriced; every side looks about fair, minus fees. Value appears only where your chance is higher than the break-even chance. So put your own % in the YOUR % column, and Katy Wins ranks bets by expected return per dollar.

    
A 95¢ "sure thing" risks 95¢ to win 5¢. One loss wipes out 19 wins. High chance ≠ best bet.

    
Fee: Kalshi's general rate is 7% × price × (1 − price) per contract, rounded up to the cent. Some markets use a different rate; change it below the table if needed.

04 // TRACK RECORD

    
Save picks, then mark them WON or LOST when they settle. Tap PAPER to switch a pick to REAL if you actually bought it, and enter the price you really paid. This shows whether your % beats the market, which is the only real proof of an edge. Picks are saved in this browser only, so back them up with the CSV button.
